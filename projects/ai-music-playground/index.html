AI Music Playground

A private workspace for experiments with AI-generated music.

The AI Music Playground is a private project for trying music-generation ideas, comparing outputs, and keeping the experiments in one place. It is not presented as a finished product or a public service.

The repository is kept private while the work remains exploratory. The public project list records the project and its status without exposing unfinished implementation details.
