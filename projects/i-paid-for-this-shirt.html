I Paid For This Shirt

A storefront that prints the exact price paid on the shirt.

open the site

The site lets a customer choose a price, then produces a shirt whose printed price matches the amount paid. The price is the central product detail rather than a hidden checkout value.

The source repository is private. The live site is the public artifact and the project is kept in the portfolio as a small commerce experiment.
