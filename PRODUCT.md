# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People evaluating Eric Spencer's research, software, writing, and public work: potential collaborators, employers, classmates, researchers, and curious visitors. They need to understand what a project is, see evidence of it, and reach the relevant paper, demo, repository, or contact link quickly.

## Product Purpose

The site is Eric Spencer's public portfolio and research index. It connects his identity to formal-methods and LLM research, software projects, publications, applications, and work history. Success means a visitor can identify the current work, understand its status and limits, and move to its primary artifact without sorting through duplicated pages or generic portfolio copy.

## Brand Personality

Technical, candid, curious. The voice is specific and conversational where the page is personal, and neutral and evidence-led where it describes AI-related work or research. The site should feel authored and considered, not like a product-marketing template.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic landing-page sections, identical card grids, gradient heroes, glass panels, purple SaaS palettes, decorative icon rows, inflated claims, and copy that narrates SEO or implementation mechanics. Do not publish archival mirrors as competing copies of the same page.

## Design Principles

- Show the artifact, result, or evidence before the category label.
- Keep one clear canonical path for each public page.
- Let typography and whitespace create hierarchy; do not fill space with decoration.
- State status, scope, and limitations plainly.
- Make the next useful link obvious on every public surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical. Preserve semantic headings, meaningful link text, descriptive image alt text, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and touch targets on narrow screens. Respect reduced-motion preferences and do not make essential information depend on client-side scripts.
