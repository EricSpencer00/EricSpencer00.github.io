Brian Ortiz Presents Poster at GCASR 2025¶

Brian Ortiz presented a work-in-progress poster at the 12th Greater Chicago Area Systems Research Workshop (GCASR 2025), held on May 8, 2025 at Loyola University Chicago’s Water Tower Campus.

The poster, co-authored with Mohammed Abuhamad, TaiNing Wang, George K. Thiruvathukal, and Konstantin Läufer, presents an automated pipeline for synthesizing TLA+ specifications from natural language using large language models, validated with the SANY parser and TLC model checker.
