About

Graduate researcher at Loyola University Chicago, working at the intersection of formal methods and large language models. Member of Technical Staff at HorneSci.

At the AI4FM / FMitF group under Prof. Konstantin Läufer: first systematic evaluation of LLM-generated TLA+, the chattla-20b model, and a paper at ICSOFT 2026. Outside the lab: the Resilient compiler, macOS and iOS apps, and FROM AMERICA LLC.
