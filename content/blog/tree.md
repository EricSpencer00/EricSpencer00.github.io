"All the branches of a tree at every stage of its height when put together are equal in thickness to the trunk below them." -[Da Vinci's Rule of Trees](link).  

You have probably had this phenomenon when vibe coding. The initial burst of productivity that happens on first turnaround of a project feels amazing. You think "Wow, this is awesome and only has a few things I'd like to change before publishing." But you don't know what you don't know. 

You think in your head, "80% done, just need to finish the last 20% and I can release this." But then you come to find out that the last 20% is actually the hardest part. 

You realize that the hardest part is the last 20% of the project. You find the browser doesn't hydrate your login correctly, the UI has a weird relationship with the settings page, the product works but not well enough, etc. AI will give you the 80% easily, then you must wrangle with it to accomplish the last 20%. Most of the time you think its just an issue with how you prompted the agent and try to greenfield another attempt with the first iteration's issues accounted for.
